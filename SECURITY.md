# Security & Privacy Policy

## 🛡️ Responsible Disclosure
NeedRadar coordinates sensitive citizen assistance, humanitarian goods, and community infrastructure reports. We take security vulnerabilities and personal privacy leaks extremely seriously.

### Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security issue or privacy concern:
1. **Do not** file a public GitHub issue.
2. Email our maintainers directly at: `samir4khan555@gmail.com` with the subject `[SECURITY VULNERABILITY] - NeedRadar`.
3. Include:
   - Steps to reproduce the issue.
   - Affected API routes or frontend pages.
   - Proof-of-concept payload or screenshot if applicable.

We will acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and provide a timeline for triage and remediation.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Minimisation Rules
- **No Direct Identity Storage**: NeedRadar never mandates or stores government identity numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, SSN) for basic community participation.
- **Location Obfuscation**: Requests marked as sensitive or individual-level automatically apply randomized spatial jitter (~300m) to protect domestic premises on the public discovery map.
- **Immutable Auditing**: All administrative decisions (approving, hiding, rejecting, verifying providers, merging duplicates) are recorded with timestamps, actor roles, and diffs.
